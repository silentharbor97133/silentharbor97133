# Hello, I'm silentharbor97133

I spend most of my time writing Python scripts that make repetitive tasks disappear. Whether it's batch-processing logs, orchestrating backups, or stitching together small tools that talk to each other, I prefer solutions that stay out of the way and just work.

## What I care about

**Python** is my main language. I like its clarity and the ecosystem that has grown around it. I reach for it when I need something reliable and readable rather than flashy.

**Automation** is the reason I write most of those scripts. If a process runs more than twice, I look for a way to hand it off to a machine. Cron jobs, simple daemons, and lightweight pipelines are the tools I use most often.

**Linux** is the environment I live in. I keep my machines lean, prefer the terminal over GUIs, and spend time understanding the system rather than fighting it. Package management, service configuration, and filesystem layout are things I enjoy figuring out.

**Open source** is how I learn and how I give back. Reading other people's code teaches me patterns I would never invent on my own. When I can, I contribute small fixes or documentation improvements—nothing dramatic, just the kind of work that keeps projects usable for the next person.

## Day-to-day

Most mornings start with a cup of coffee and a look at whatever is currently broken or incomplete. I keep a short list of scripts I want to improve and a longer list of ideas that may never leave the notebook. The best days are the ones where a messy manual process becomes a single command.

I am not chasing the latest framework or the hottest trend. I care more about code that still makes sense six months later and systems that require little attention once they are running.

## A few notes

- I value clear error messages and predictable behavior over clever one-liners.
- Documentation is part of the work, not an afterthought.
- The best tools are the ones you forget you are using.

If you are working on something similar—small, practical, and focused on making life a little easier—feel free to open an issue or start a discussion. I am always interested in seeing how other people solve the same problems.

Thanks for stopping by.
